Features Worth Comparing

Comfort

Consider headband adjustment, ear-cup padding, weight and fit for longer listening sessions.

Battery and Charging

Review the manufacturer's stated battery life and charging requirements.

Sound and Microphone

Check sound settings, microphone quality information and noise-control features.

Device Compatibility

Confirm that the headphones support your phone, laptop or other intended device.
