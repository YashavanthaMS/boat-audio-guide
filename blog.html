How to Choose the Right Audio Product

Choosing earbuds or headphones becomes easier when you understand your needs and compare the features that matter to you.

5 Things to Check Before Buying

1. Identify Your Main Use

Decide whether you need an audio product for travel, exercise, online classes, calls or everyday music listening.

2. Compare Comfort and Fit

Look for a design that suits your ears, head shape and typical listening duration.

3. Review Battery Life

Compare the manufacturer's stated playback time and charging options with your daily routine.

4. Check Connectivity

Make sure the product is compatible with your devices and supports the connections you need.

5. Compare Features and Budget

Compare sound features, microphone capabilities, warranty information and price before deciding.
